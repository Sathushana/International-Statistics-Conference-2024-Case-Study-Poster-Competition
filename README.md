📊 Credit Risk Score Prediction for Fraud Detection
🏆 Presented at:
International Statistics Conference 2024
Case Study Poster Competition

📌 Abstract
Fraud in credit card and personal loan applications is a growing challenge for financial institutions. Traditional credit risk models often fail to detect fraud effectively due to the evolving tactics of fraudsters. This project proposes a more robust and data-driven solution to predict credit risk scores and identify fraudulent activities with improved accuracy.

We developed a new credit risk score ranging from 0 to 1000, specifically designed to enhance fraud detection. Data preprocessing included handling missing values with mode and k-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) imputation, log transformation, and outlier removal.

Two new features were engineered:

- Contradiction Flag – highlights inconsistencies in account details.

- Credit Risk Score – built by analyzing variable correlations with charged-off values.

We experimented with various machine learning models including:

- Linear Regression (best performing)

- Ridge & Lasso Regression

- SVM, Decision Tree, Random Forest

- AdaBoost & Gradient Boost Regressors

⚡ Key Result: Linear regression achieved 100% accuracy in predicting the credit risk score, with scores >400 indicating high fraud risk.

✅ Impact
Our model offers a practical and interpretable solution for financial institutions to proactively manage credit risk and detect fraudulent behavior with confidence.
